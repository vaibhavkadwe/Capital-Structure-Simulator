Model notes

Levered beta uses Hamada: βL = βu[1 + (1 − Tc)D/E]. Cost of equity is CAPM. Cost of debt is the base rate plus a spread that rises with falling interest coverage (a synthetic-rating table adapted from large-cap coverage bands, scaled by the distress setting) and with leverage. Interest and coverage are solved iteratively. Firm value is a zero-growth perpetuity, EBIT(1 − Tc) / WACC; debt is sized as D/V × total capital. The tax shield band shows Tc×D on top of unlevered value. Educational model, not investment advice.
