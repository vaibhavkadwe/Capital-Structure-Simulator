Mature Utility

Regulated, capital-heavy, low-beta cash flows that lenders are willing to underwrite. Low distress sensitivity and a 25% tax rate make a large tax shield worth harvesting. The WACC trough is typically at a high debt ratio.

Distressed Retail

Already levered, with a high base cost of debt and earnings that barely cover interest. Extra borrowing inflates spreads quickly. The model usually recommends paying debt down rather than adding more.
