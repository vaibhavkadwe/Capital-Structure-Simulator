How the engine works

Methodology and assumptions

A transparent, educational implementation of static trade-off theory. Every formula is in the browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Steps the model takes

  1. Debt is sized as D = (D/V) × total capital. Equity is the residual so that D + E = V.
  2. Levered beta uses Hamada: βL = βu[1 + (1 − Tc)D/E]. Cost of equity is CAPM: Re = Rf + βL × ERP.
  3. Cost of debt starts at the base pre-tax rate. Interest and coverage are circular, so the engine damps for 14 iterations: coverage maps to a continuous spread, scaled by distress sensitivity, plus a cubic leverage penalty.
  4. WACC blends equity and after-tax debt. Enterprise value is a zero-growth perpetuity: EBIT(1 − Tc) / WACC, floored at a 0.5% discount rate.
  5. The engine sweeps debt ratios from 0% to 90% in 1-point steps and records the row with the lowest WACC as the simulated optimum.

Synthetic rating table

Interest coverage is mapped to a large-cap-style rating and a credit spread. Spreads are interpolated through coverage knots so the chart does not jump. The distress slider multiplies the spread above the AAA floor (0.63%) and the extra leverage penalty.

AAA ≥ 8.5x · BBB ≥ 2.5x · B ≥ 1.5x · CCC ≥ 0.8x · D below 0.2x

Badges use green (A-or-better), amber (BBB–BB), coral (single-B) and red (CCC and below) so coverage quality stays readable.

Limitations

  • No growth, no personal taxes, no agency costs, no issuance costs, and no option to default strategically.
  • Debt is a perpetual ratio of book-like total capital, not a market-value target that is rebalanced in a full APV model.
  • Spreads are synthetic. They are not quotes from a ratings agency or a bond market.
  • EBIT is a constant. Real firms have cyclical earnings, covenants and liquidity constraints the sweep does not see.
  • The “optimum” is the minimum of this particular WACC function, not a recommendation for any issuer.

This site is a teaching tool. It does not offer financial, legal or tax advice, and it should not be used to size real-world capital structures, buy or sell securities, or substitute for a credit opinion.
